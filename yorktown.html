01The objective

The client wanted to launch a premium tier that combined its goals-based investing with human advice — targeting investors aged 26–55 with $100K–$2M+ in investable assets.

The question we needed to answer: which features actually justified the upgrade, and which investor segments were most likely to convert? Generic robo-advice felt insufficient, but full traditional advisory was too expensive and too high-touch. The brief was to find the right hybrid.

02What we did

Three rounds of qualitative research with 30+ target investors across three psychographic segments — Unsure Procrastinators, Intermediate Experimenters, and Advanced Over-Extenders — surfaced the underlying motivations for each.

A quantitative study used forced-choice tradeoff exercises to prioritize 16 functional benefits and rank feature desirability — producing a build-ready feature hierarchy backed by data.

We tested 6 hero feature categories through prototype validation, built a competitive positioning model, facilitated design workshops on advisor matching and family financial views, and developed detailed persona briefs documenting prospecting triggers and product implications.

03The solution

Guidevest — a hybrid digital/human wealth product built on three validated pillars: Expert Human Advice, Personalized Goals-based Investing, and a Premium Digital Experience.

Expert Human Advice anchors the offer with access to a pool of financial advisors, advisor matching, scheduling, screensharing, and post-appointment reviews. Personalized Goals-based Investing combines custom portfolios with goal tracking and risk adjustment. The Premium Digital Experience layers on an advanced financial wellness assessment, a digital family view, an equity award diagnostic tool, retirement preparedness, and account aggregation.

Each feature was carried into the build with research-backed "where there is interest" and "what to consider" notes — so engineering and design knew not just what to build, but why it mattered to which segment and where the open questions still sat.
