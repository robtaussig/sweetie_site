01The objective

SoFi saw an opening in small-business banking — 44% of US GDP, with 57% of SMBs open to new banking solutions — yet root needs around guidance and time remained unmet by incumbents and digital-first players alike.

We were brought in to answer four venture-board questions with rigor: will members adopt, can we win, will it make money, and can we build it?

02What we did

We interviewed 28 potential members across four regions and three personas — Solopreneurs, Wayfinding Employers, and Established Employers — plus nine competitive experts. A 744-respondent quant study (527 prospects, 217 existing SoFi members) validated desirability at scale.

An interactive Figma prototype with 8 core features and 5 investment deep-dives surfaced real user reactions, while a risk burndown framework mapped what we knew, what we assumed, and what needed continued testing against the four venture-board questions.

We modeled a five-year financial plan (base, bull, bear) projecting 144K members, $310M revenue, and $92M pretax income by Year 5 — and scoped the Minimum Marketable Product into four cross-functional squads: Sign-up & Onboarding, Core Banking, Lending, and Expert Team.

If everything could be in one dedicated place, that would be major. It would make everything streamlined so I can stop dealing with day-to-day finances and focus on growing my business.
Peter C. · Wayfinding Employer
03The solution

A three-phase build sequenced by segment readiness and revenue unlock. The Minimum Marketable Product launches with Solopreneurs and early-adopter employers — foundational banking with personalized support: spending and savings accounts with competitive interest, a Business Credit-Building Card, Adaptive Company Cards, an Expert Team for bankers, Customer 360 AI insights, and a Small Loan Marketplace via Lantern.

Post-MMP serves Wayfinding Employers with a rewards business credit card, Instant Cash short-term lending, Small Business Lending via white label, an AI-driven retention tool, and OneStep expense approval. The Destination state extends to Established Employers with expanded banking, AI Risk Monitoring for bankers, Money Maximizer liquidity optimization, and SoFi planner referrals — positioning Jethro as the horizontal financial partner for SMBs.
