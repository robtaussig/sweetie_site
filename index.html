About

Strategy, grounded
in people.

Andrea is a product and strategy leader with a foundation in human-centered design. She guides venture teams through innovation, validation, and incubation — helping translate ambiguous problems into meaningful products, services, and businesses that reach customers.

With 15+ years spanning agency and consultancy work, she has led projects across financial services, retail, lifestyle, and consumer brands. She thrives at the intersection of strategy, consumer insight, and usability, and is known for mentoring the multidisciplinary teams that build alongside her.

Her work has earned industry recognition including a Gold Clio, a Facebook Studio Award, and NASCA Innovation Awards.
